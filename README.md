# New Page

i often used 'new tab' extentions which gave option to choose one search engine at a time, you can change it , but its not too handy,,,

and also i did not found any 'new tab' extention wich came featured with a calender. 

---
this 'new tab' offers you,,
<ul>
    <li><b><i>A clock</i></b> (ofcourse everyone needs one)</li>
    <li><b><i>multiple search</i></b> (google, brave, duckduckgo, perplixcity, youtube...)</li>
    <li> scrool down and you will find <b><i> A news box,</i></b> (fetches news from mit rss feed, it updates daily)</li>
    <li><b><i>a new background in news box everyday</i></b> (best pic of the day by Nasa)</li>
    <li><b><i>And a Calender</i></b> (build from scratch)</li>
</ul>
